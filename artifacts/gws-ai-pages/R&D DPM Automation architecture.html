Core Business Rules & Mathematical Integrity

Rule 1: True Weighted Average

Group Average is never an arithmetic average of factory DPMs. It equals the grand total defects divided by total gloves inspected across all plants:

(Total Defects / Total Inspected) * 1,000,000

Rule 2: Red Highlighting (#FF0000)

In Slide 1, Slide 5, and the Validation Dashboard:

If (Plant DPM > Group Avg DPM) ➔ BOLD RED
Else ➔ Standard Dark Font

Rule 3: Bottom 3 Root Causes

For Slides 2–4 (NR & NBR) and Slide 6 (Surgical):

1. Rank plants by Total DPM desc
2. Filter categories > Group Avg
3. Extract MAX defect code & name

📁 Master Trackers at Root vs Subfolders Rationale

Why Master Trackers Stay at Root

The 3 rolling master trackers (Monthly NR DPM Data, Monthly NBR DPM Data, Monthly Surgical DPM Data) continuously append new month columns (Jan through Dec).

If they were moved inside 10. Oct26/, subsequent runs for 11. Nov26 and 12. Dec26 would lose continuity and fail to locate the rolling master.

What Belongs in Monthly Subfolders

Subfolders (1. Jan26 – 12. Dec26) hold month-specific execution packages:

  • Raw PowerBI Exports: DPM - NR (Month).xlsx / Google Sheet
  • Final Executive Presentations: NR & Surgical DPM for Month (Master)
  • NBR Master Deck: NBR DPM for Month (Master)

The Bilateral Guardrails (Why the Score is Trustworthy)

Claims, Not Facts

Every finding is verified against primary sources before action. ~30% of raw claims fail verification.

Changes ≠ Fixes

Author fix batches are independently audited for regressions before subsequent re-evaluations.

Refuted Ledger

Disproven claims are recorded in REFUTED-LEDGER.md with evidence so dead ideas are never re-raised.

Domain Authority

The critic proposes and tests; it never mutates validated domains directly. The domain gauntlet is the improver.
