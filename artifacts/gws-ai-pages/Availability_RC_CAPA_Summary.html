Root Cause Taxonomy & Engineering Failure Modes Categorical Breakdown
1. Former Ratio & Product Changeover Overhaul (67.8% of Declarations)
Dominant Cause: Lines F4, F33, F36, F18B, and F27 regularly spend 8 to 24 hours down to switch weights, colors (e.g. CW55 Natural → CW58 Black, ENW 50 → ENW 70), and adjust former size ratios.
Common Operations: Simultaneous tank washing (chlorine, coagulant, latex), acid dipping, and oven cleaning.
Pattern Observed: Upline preparation frequently suffers delays from slow former alignment and cleaning bottlenecks.
2. Thermal Energy & Boiler Systems Catastrophic Risk (5 Breakdowns)
Critical Incidents:
• F36 (L10S, 25/09): Total factory stop! Conveyor 3 liner worn & torn → chain jammed → scrapper plate broke → boiler forced stop.
• F41 (L1D, 01/10): Boiler 1 failed to reach thermal oil setpoint; forced line stop before switching to standby boiler 2.
• F7 (L3D, 14/10): PEA grid trip combined with boiler switching failure due to refractory partition leaking.
3. Conveyor Line, Oven Bearing & Drive Mechanics (6 Breakdowns)
Mechanical Failures:
• F33 (L15D & L13D & L14D): Multiple chain-outs at latex oven due to stuck holders, latex oven bearing damage, and main drive motor failure.
• F4 (L12S, 18/10): Chain-out caused by excessive chain slackness and tensioning box defect.
• F36 (L10S, 20/09): Jammed gearbox and jammed chain conveyor causing delayed upline.
• F32 (L4D, 19/09): Sprocket misalignment at latex oven and broken formers.
4. Automation, Utilities & External Disruptions (4 Incidents)
• F13 (L12D, 12-13/09): Chiller motor failure causing 18 hours of downtime for compounding/latex cooling.
• F33 (L7D, 12/09): RASM (Robotic Automatic Stripping Machine) controller bearing failure.

• F2 (L10D, 07-08/10): Plant IT fiber-optic disconnection causing low reporting availability.

Evaluation of corrective and preventive actions declared by factory managers and engineers.

✓ Effective & Systemic CAPAs
  • TPM Consolidation: 28 of 40 planned declarations successfully bundled tank cleaning, conveyor track nylon replacement, and sprocket turning into single stops.
  • Chemical Optimization: F33 studied alternative cleaning chemicals to cut off-dip former cleaning duration.
  • Chemistry Tuning: F38 tuned PF coagulant & powder concentration to prevent former fouling.
× Generic / Weak CAPAs to Eliminate
  • "Production, planner and maintenance team to plan and coordinate well" (Repeated verbatim in 8 F4 submissions without technical countermeasure).
  • "Train contract workers to work well in planned downtime" (Shifts root cause to labor rather than process design).
  • "Check carefully the actual root cause" (Deferred investigation; not an action plan).
Recommended Engineering Actions

1. SMED Changeover Standard: Implement Single-Minute Exchange of Die (SMED) across F33, F4, F36. Pre-stage formers, pre-mix chemicals, and standardize upline sequence to target ≤6h for full product conversions.

2. Predictive Boiler & Thermal Audits: Institute weekly refractory inspections and conveyor liner thickness checks at F36, F41, and F7 to eliminate multi-line thermal shutoffs.

3. Oven Chain & Bearing Pre-Emption: Standardize high-temperature bearing replacements and ultrasonic vibration testing on F33 latex oven tracks.

4. Strict CAPA Rejection Policy: Central engineering team should reject submissions with generic "plan and coordinate well" text, requiring specific technical mitigations.
