Framework Mandate

The 5-Pillar Proposed Management Agreement

A comprehensive top-down mandate to eliminate shadow IT, centralize knowledge assets, and establish an AI-ready digital enterprise foundation.

P1

GWS as Preferred Platform

Google Workspace becomes the sole authorized environment for official document creation, communication, and collaboration under unified SSO and Context-Aware Access.

P2

Google Chat Migration

Official messaging transitions completely from consumer apps (WhatsApp) to Google Chat Spaces featuring in-line threading, DLP protection, and Google Vault archiving.

P3

Corporate Shared Drives

Deprecate individual "My Drive" storage for team projects. Files belong to organizational Shared Drives with standardized folder taxonomies and group permissions.

P4

Data Governance & Rules

Deploy up to 150 Drive classification labels, badged security tags, and DLP rules to prevent external leakage and constrain AI RAG search boundaries.

P5

Phased Departmental Pilot

Initiate migration within selected high-collaboration units (IT, Operations, Marketing) to refine DLP rules, tune Space templates, and train champions before enterprise rollout.

International Governance Framework Mapping

Standardizing GWS aligns IT operations directly with international standards for corporate governance, information security, and AI risk management.

ISO/IEC 38500 & COBIT

Corporate IT Governance
  • APO14 (Managed Data): Converts dark, unstructured data into categorized assets in Shared Drives.
  • DSS05 (Security): Centralizes identity-based perimeter control via SAML 2.0 SSO.

ISO/IEC 27001:2022

Information Security
  • A.5.10 (AUP): Formally replaces WhatsApp with mandate for Chat/Drive.
  • A.8.12 (DLP): Workspace DLP rules block external leakage of confidential data.
  • A.8.15 (Logging): Tamper-evident Vault logs satisfy compliance requirements.

ISO 42001 & NIST AI RMF

AI Risk Management
  • AI Data Inputs: Guarantees Gemini operates exclusively on authenticated corporate context.
  • Bounded RAG: Enforces Principal Access Boundaries to block unauthorized AI retrieval.
