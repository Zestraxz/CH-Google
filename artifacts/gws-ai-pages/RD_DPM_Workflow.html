How Monthly DPM Raw Exports Reach Trackers, Validation Audits & Executive Master Decks

Each month, PowerBI raw DPM exports for NR, NBR & Surgical are processed into integer metrics, sorted descending by Total DPM, and audited with =MAX() defect formulas. The pipeline synchronizes the Monthly Trackers, updates the DPM Automation Controller, clones the official Google Slides master templates, and exports 100% formatted PowerPoint (.pptx) presentations ready for executive review.

1. Local Python Engine Workflow

Monthly Execution · Python 3.10+ / OpenPyXL / Python-PPTX

  1. PowerBI Ingestion: Ingests raw export files for NR, NBR, and Surgical. Verifies that all 10 mandatory PowerBI filters match the QA specification.
  2. Integer Formatting: Applies mathematical rounding (round()) and number format #,##0 to all DPM metrics. No decimals are stored or displayed.
  3. High DPM Sorting: Sorts factory rows descending by overall Total DPM so the highest defect factory sits at the top (Row 2).
  4. Top 3 Yellow Highlighting: Evaluates the top 3 worst-performing factories (highest DPM) and applies bright yellow fill (#FFFF00) to Column A with bold text across Total and all category tabs.
  5. Granular QA Tabs: Generates intermediate tabs (Major, Minor, NFC, Pinhole) with dynamic formula =MAX(B3:AI3) in Column AJ to pinpoint each factory's peak defect.
  6. Tracker Synchronization: Appends the new target month column into Monthly NR/NBR/Surgical DPM Data.xlsx, properly handling Pinhole's historical column offset.
  7. PowerPoint Master Generation: Clones local master decks, populates the 13-column executive summary tables (Slide 1 & Slide 5), marks numbers exceeding group average in bold red (#FF0000), updates the Top 3 Lowest DPM remarks, and generates the Bottom 3 Factory Pareto action plan slides.

2. Google Cloud Apps Script Workflow

Google Drive · DPM Automation Controller · Code.gs

  1. Drive Auto-Detection: Script detects its folder location (Production: 1vBX43PyWWhhMAG1YXZcqDEQCTrfRxgfR or Test: 1Y-7byz1vyhuzRzddfDHEPkWJ1C7Wc-i3) and accesses/creates numbered month subfolders (1. Jan26 to 12. Dec26).
  2. Excel/PPTX Conversion: Automatically converts uploaded .xlsx and .pptx files to native Google Sheets and Google Slides formats via Drive API.
  3. QA Audit & Slide Summary Tabs: Rebuilds Validation_Summary (full audit view with formula comparisons) and Slide_Summary (13-column executive format with RED styling) in the controller spreadsheet.
  4. Master Template Cloning (100% Identical): Instead of blank slides, the script clones the official master Google Slides templates:
    • NBR Template: 1o-y53Ss_OS76kNNaggKgg98XUJRFPtdq
    • NR & Surgical Template: 1IsEZL_Hhz6fnLLvsk5DFKF0pyxtXZDeL
  5. Executive Table Population: Populates the 18x13 (NBR), 19x13 (NR), and 6x13 (Surgical) tables cell-by-cell. Formats month labels (FY, Prev, Curr), and applies red bold text to any metric higher than the Category Group Average.
  6. Remarks & Pareto Update: Recalculates the Top 3 lowest total DPM factories and writes the official Remarks box. Updates Bottom Factory slides with subtitle In <Month> and top defects.
  7. PowerPoint (.pptx) Export: Exports both cloned presentations as .pptx files into the monthly folder using OAuth token and UrlFetchApp, ready for immediate download.

Quality Assurance & Data Integrity Rules

RuleStandard SpecificationImplementation & Validation
Zero Decimals All DPM metrics must be rounded integers with comma separators (e.g. 32,936, not 32936.45) Enforced Math.round(val) + NumberFormat #,##0 applied in Python & Apps Script.
High DPM Ranking Factory rows must be sorted descending by Total DPM (highest defect factory at top) Enforced factoryRows.sort((a,b) => b.total - a.total) before writing to sheets.
Top 3 High DPM Highlight Top 3 worst factories (highest total DPM) must be highlighted in Column A Yellow Cell background set to #FFFF00 with bold text on Row 2, 3, 4.
Red Text Exceeding Average In Slide 1 / Slide 5 tables, any factory metric greater than the Category Group Average must be styled RED #DC2626 Applied to Current & Previous month columns when val > groupAvg.
Top 3 Lowest DPM Remarks Slide 1 remarks box must name the Top 3 best factories (lowest non-zero total DPM) Enforced Filtered non-zero, sorted ascending, and formatted as e.g. F10, F8A & F18B.
Granular Defect Audit Category sheets (Major, Minor, NFC, Pinhole) must show highest individual defect code and value Enforced Column AJ injected with formula =MAX(B3:AI3) matching raw PowerBI export.
